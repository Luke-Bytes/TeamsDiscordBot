## Mobility and infiltration classes

**Acrobat** has a double-jump ability, useful for crossing gaps or escaping. **Scout** trades on mobility with a grapple and a speed bonus; a combat tag prevents grappling briefly after being hit. **Transporter** places linked teleport portals to move teammates strategically. **Assassin** is an infiltration-oriented choice for Nexus attacks. **Spy**, **Ninja**, **Dasher**, **Rift Walker** and **Neptune** are other movement, stealth or traversal choices; check each class's own wiki page before giving exact powers or timings. Sources: https://wiki.shotbow.net/Acrobat/en ; https://wiki.shotbow.net/Scout ; https://wiki.shotbow.net/Transporter ; https://wiki.shotbow.net/Annihilation_Classes

## Mining, building and utility classes

**Miner** improves ore gathering, including chances for extra ore and a Gold Rush ability. **Enchanter** gains additional XP levels from resource gathering and can boost nearby teammates' XP gain. **Builder** supports defensive construction. **Farmer** benefits from harvesting crops. **Handyman** has a chance to repair its own Nexus while dealing damage to an enemy Nexus. Other utility options from the roster include **Alchemist, Engineer, Lumberjack, Tinkerer, Bard** and **Healer**. They fill different resource, support or defensive niches; exact abilities differ. Sources: https://wiki.shotbow.net/Miner ; https://wiki.shotbow.net/Enchanter ; https://wiki.shotbow.net/Builder ; https://wiki.shotbow.net/Handyman

## Ranged, combat and control classes

**Archer** is a ranged bow-focused class with an infinite-arrow mechanic in its published wiki entry. **Healer** specialises in restoring allies' health and removing negative effects. **Swapper** can swap positions with another player and is used for displacement and traps. **Scorpio** and **Immobilizer** are notable control-oriented picks. **Warrior, Berserker, Bloodmage, Pyro, Defender, Hunter, Mercenary, Robin Hood, Sniper, Spider, Succubus, Tank, Thor, Vampire, Wizard** and others provide distinct PvP, defensive or ranged styles. ## Choosing Annihilation classes in a team

For a **Nexus rush**, mobility or stealth helps an attacker reach a Nexus, but support and coordinated gear usually matter more than a single “best class.” For **defense**, prioritise positions, communication and complementary control/building classes. For **early economy**, Miner/Enchanter/Farmer are natural areas to investigate; for **team travel**, look at Transporter. Class matchup claims and “best build” rankings are context-specific, not fixed facts. In FW, ask which classes are currently banned using FW commands/tools rather than assuming public Shotbow's allowed roster. Source: https://wiki.shotbow.net/Annihilation_Classes

For someone asking “best kit for rushing”, **kit** means an Annihilation class and **rushing** means attacking the enemy Nexus, not using guns. Scout (grapple/mobility), Acrobat (double jump), Assassin (infiltration) and Transporter (team portals) are sensible classes to discuss depending on the rush plan; there is no universal best. For “best class for defending”, consider Swapper or Immobilizer for control, Builder for structures, and Healer for support. Defending means protecting your Nexus. These are tactical suggestions based on the documented class roles, not official rankings.
