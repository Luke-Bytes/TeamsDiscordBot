# Public Shotbow Annihilation basics for Navi

Official reference: https://wiki.shotbow.net/Annihilation_Game_Summary . Checked November 2026. These rules describe Shotbow's public Annihilation mode, not necessarily the FW Discord match format.

## Annihilation objective and teams

**Annihilation** is a team-based Minecraft PvP game about destroying the opposing **Nexuses** while protecting your own. The usual public mode has **Red, Blue, Yellow and Green** teams occupying different map quadrants. Each team gathers materials, enchants gear, builds defenses, contests middle, and launches coordinated attacks. The **last team still possessing a Nexus wins**; teams whose Nexus is destroyed cannot respawn, though surviving players can continue fighting. Source: https://wiki.shotbow.net/Annihilation_Game_Summary

A normal Nexus begins with **75 health** on the public wiki; mining it reduces health and triggers warning sounds. A destroyed Nexus removes that team's respawns, not every remaining player immediately. **Duel Mode** uses just **Red and Blue** and compatible two-team maps, intended to start games with fewer players; it was introduced in March 2024. These are Shotbow mechanics, not FW's match-persistence or winner-reporting logic. Sources: https://wiki.shotbow.net/Annihilation_Game_Summary ; https://shotbow.net/forum/threads/annihilation-update-duel-mode-3-3-0.423826/

## Annihilation phases and resource timing

The wiki describes **five phases**, normally **10 minutes each**, with Phase 5 continuing until a winner: **Phase 1** - gather and defend (Nexuses invulnerable); **Phase 2** - Nexus damage becomes possible; **Phase 3** - diamonds appear at middle and witches appear; **Phase 4** - boss portals open for a chance at a team boss reward, and blaze powder becomes purchasable; **Phase 5** - Nexus damage doubles and a Netherite Sword becomes available in the shop. Don't mistake “Phase 5 lasts 10 minutes” for an automatic end. Source: https://wiki.shotbow.net/Annihilation_Game_Summary

## Annihilation economy, bosses and preparation

Public games reward efficient early mining and enchanting, coordinated defense, mid control after diamonds appear, and timed Nexus rushes. A team's **Ender Furnace** is private to each user of that team and smelts faster than ordinary furnaces. In Phase 4, killing a boss and claiming its buff unlocks a special item shop for the team. A base **Nether Portal** allows class switching. Boss mechanics and loot have changed in patches, especially **Rising Radium (4.2.0)**, so defer to current official wiki/changelogs for exact cooldowns, items or damage numbers. Sources: https://wiki.shotbow.net/Annihilation_Game_Summary ; https://wiki.shotbow.net/Annihilation_Bosses

## Annihilation advice and version caveats

A sensible public-game plan is: pick complementary classes, gather iron and levels early, prepare defenses before Phase 2, contest diamonds in Phase 3, organise boss teams in Phase 4, and coordinate attacks or counter-rushes when Phase 5 doubles Nexus damage. These are general tactics, not absolute requirements or claims about live teams. Annihilation's Minecraft base version changes over time (for example, the **February 2026 base 1.21.10** update), so older class guides and item descriptions can be stale.
