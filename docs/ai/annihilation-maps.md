# Public Annihilation maps for Navi

Primary list: https://wiki.shotbow.net/Annihilation_Maps . Checked November 2026. This is the public Shotbow catalog, **not** the selected map or legal map enum in an FW match.

## Annihilation map pool and rotation

Shotbow's map wiki says a **10-map rotation** is picked, with the **live pool visible in the Annihilation lobby**. Public games offer **four maps** from that rotation for voting before a game; players can change their vote. Its tier scheme is: **Tier 1** always in rotation, **Tier 2** community favourites, **Tier 3** randomly selected, and **Tier 4** new maps. Seasonal skins can replace regular variants. The wiki page is a catalog/snapshot, not a real-time rotation lookup. Source: https://wiki.shotbow.net/Annihilation_Maps

## Classic Annihilation maps and favourites

**Coastal** is the Tier 1 evergreen classic on the published wiki. Tier 2 community favourites listed there are **Aftermath 2.0, Andorra 3.9.2, Canyon, Cherokee, Hamlet, Kingdom, Nature and Villages**. These are public Shotbow rotation categories, not a guarantee that a specific FW event can play them. The tiers are **not an official competitive ranking** or proof of match popularity; for a request like “top 3 maps for competitive Anni”, give a clearly subjective shortlist rather than claiming an official top three or live rotation. Source: https://wiki.shotbow.net/Annihilation_Maps

## Notable Annihilation map details

**Coastal** is the perennial reference map. **Aftermath 2.0** was added in July 2015 and draws on MineZ landmarks. **AnniZ** was made from sections of the MineZ world. **Hamlet** places its Nexus below its central building. **Kingdom's** middle is based on SMASH's Cathedral design. **Rifts** has bridges linking middle and lower areas. **Temples** is water-heavy, which changes movement and defending choices. **Ironvale** was added in May 2025, **Willowmere** in August 2025.
