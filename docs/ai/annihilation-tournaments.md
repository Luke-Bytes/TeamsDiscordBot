# Shotbow Annihilation clan tournament history for Navi

Research checked 22 November 2026 against Shotbow forum registration threads, official monthly recaps and Shotbow match videos. This is **public Shotbow Annihilation history**, not the Friendly Wars Discord bot's own events. A clan listed in a _registration post_ is not automatically proven to have played; only award a finishing position when a result or final match identifies it. No complete, official all-clan standings table was found for most editions.

## Annihilation tournament champions and runners-up

- **2023:** **DDD** won; **Agency** finished **second**. Shotbow's 1 August 2023 recap names both finalists and DDD as champion. The final aired **22 July 2023**, on a special **Andorra 1v1** map. Sources: https://shotbow.net/forum/threads/dentrils-new-moon-august-2023.421489/ and https://youtube.com/watch?v=FA9PNNueFjc
- **2024:** **NyRq** won; **Rawr v2** finished **second**. Shotbow's 1 June recap names the final pairing, and the 2 July recap confirms NyRq's victory. Sources: https://shotbow.net/forum/threads/dentrils-new-moon-june-2024.425314/ and https://shotbow.net/forum/threads/dentrils-new-moon-july-2024.425674/
- **2026:** **Aura (AURA)** won; **DONTCOMEANNI (#Annl / ANNL)** finished **second**. Shotbow announced Aura's victory **3 September 2026**; its official final video, published **30 August 2026**, is titled "ANNL vs AURA". Sources: https://shotbow.net/forum/portal/?page=2 and https://youtube.com/watch?v=zXGzDq8b5nU

These are **confirmed first and second places**, not complete rankings. Third place and below are **unverified** without a published bracket or explicit result. A tournament's announcement date is not necessarily its finish date.

## Annihilation Clan Tournament 2023: entrants and format

**Announced 23 January 2023** for February–March play, later running through July. The official format was **10 active players and up to 3 substitutes per clan**, with initial **1v1 group-stage** matches followed by knockouts. The sign-up thread contains these **named applicants**, beyond finalists DDD and Agency: **Frontier [FRNT]**, **KGB**, **01**, **F3 Entity** and **Ace**. All seven have posted rosters in the thread; exact attendance, eliminations and placements below second were **not established** by these registration posts. Source: https://shotbow.net/forum/threads/annihilation-clan-tournament-2023.419233/

Known named posted participants/applicants by result: **DDD — first**; **Agency — second**; **Frontier, KGB, 01, F3 Entity, Ace — registered, final positions unknown**. A post simply commenting "#TMC" is **not evidence** of confirmed entry. The official **1 August 2023** recap settles the final result, not every intermediate place. Sources: https://shotbow.net/forum/threads/annihilation-clan-tournament-2023.419233/ and https://shotbow.net/forum/threads/dentrils-new-moon-august-2023.421489/

## Annihilation Clan Tournament 2024: entrants and format

**Announced 9 December 2023** for matches beginning in 2024. The format again used **10 starters and 3 substitutes**, groups then knockout rounds. New class bans meant **Swapper** was universally banned and each captain could ban one further class before a match. The announcement thread explicitly includes roster posts from **Rawr v2**, **The Family** and **AZUL**. **NyRq** is additionally confirmed as a finalist/champion by official recaps. Sources: https://shotbow.net/forum/threads/annihilation-clan-tournament-2024.422851/ and https://shotbow.net/forum/threads/dentrils-new-moon-july-2024.425674/

Shotbow posted recordings of games involving **TP**, **MRK**, and **PoiuYT** (as _match-title labels_, not verified full clan names). The official 2024 recordings include **TP vs MRK, 14 February 2024**, and **TP vs PoiuYT, 7 April 2024**; their match titles establish play but **not a final placement**. **Rawr v2 — second; NyRq — first; The Family, AZUL, TP, MRK, PoiuYT — standings unknown**. Some labels may refer to the same organisation; do not double-count or equate "TP" with "The Family" without confirmation. Sources: https://youtube.com/watch?v=0REO1RSfOjE and https://youtube.com/watch?v=UoSDfAWjvDY

The tournament was still in semifinals by the **1 June 2024** staff recap. The final featured **Rawr v2 vs NyRq**; the **2 July 2024** recap announced **NyRq as champion**. No supported third-place ranking was established from these summaries. Sources: https://shotbow.net/forum/threads/dentrils-new-moon-june-2024.425314/ and https://shotbow.net/forum/threads/dentrils-new-moon-july-2024.425674/

## Annihilation Tournament 2026: format and confirmed finalists

**Announced 25 March 2026**; the official registration deadline was **26 April 2026**. One clan submitted a leader plus 12 players, with **up to 10 players per team in each 1v1**. This edition changed Nexus health, Phase 5 and class bans during different tournament stages. Its announced prizes distinguished first/second from participation rewards. Sources: https://shotbow.net/forum/threads/annihilation-2026-tournament.432532/

**Winner: Aura** (listed in sign-ups as **A U R A**); **runner-up: DONTCOMEANNI**, shown with the tag **#Annl** and abbreviated **ANNL** in the official final video. The final video appeared **30 August 2026**; Shotbow's **3 September 2026** newsletter confirms Aura won. Do not confuse **ANNL** with the Annihilation game abbreviation _Anni_. Sources: https://youtube.com/watch?v=zXGzDq8b5nU and https://shotbow.net/forum/portal/?page=2

## Annihilation Spring 2018 tournament completed in 2019

Shotbow's **Spring 2018 Tournament** began in **March 2018**, drew **48 teams expressing interest**, and had its last quarterfinal played in June. Its delayed final was held **26 January 2019**; the February 2019 official report calls it the **Annihilation 21st Century Tournament**. This is **one event**, not separate 2018 and 2019 annual championships. The accessible recaps confirm completion but **do not establish the champion or full final standings**. Source: https://shotbow.net/forum/threads/the-year-ahead-2019-dentrils-new-moon-february-2019.392056/

The 2018 bracket discussion mentions **Oblivion**, **Team Turkish**, **japanesesnipers**, **Gov**, and **Team Envy**. It records **Team Turkish's disqualification** in a quarterfinal and **Team Envy's disqualification** after a match; **Gov** could not field a team for an earlier fixture. These are _historical bracket/report mentions_, not a confirmed exhaustive attendance list or podium. Sources: https://shotbow.net/forum/threads/the-annihilation-spring-2018-tournament-brackets.384112/page-3 ; https://shotbow.net/forum/threads/the-annihilation-spring-2018-tournament-brackets.384112/page-4 ; https://shotbow.net/forum/threads/the-annihilation-spring-2018-tournament-brackets.384112/page-5

## Other Shotbow clan tournaments and missing years

The **Team Nexus** community-hosted clan war seasons are **not interchangeable** with Shotbow's official 2023/2024/2026 championship. Its forum history records **Spring 2020: TeamTurkish**, **Summer & Fall 2020: rawr**, and a **Mushroom Tournament: Mizu**. The Mushroom event's year is **not confirmed** by that winner list; do not automatically date it 2020. Source: https://shotbow.net/forum/threads/team-nexus-discord-server-for-clans-and-community-events.384901/

No separate **Shotbow-organised** 2025 Annihilation Clan Tournament result was established in the reviewed forum announcements. That means **unknown/unverified**, not "cancelled" or "there was no tournament." The separately run **2025 Friendly Wars community tournament** has a reported result, recorded in the approved `docs/ai/friendly-wars-tournaments.md` guide; it is **not** evidence of a 2025 Shotbow staff championship. Do not invent a winner for the 2018-started event or claim this list covers every year since Annihilation began. Sources for the separate Shotbow editions: https://shotbow.net/forum/threads/annihilation-clan-tournament-2024.422851/ and https://shotbow.net/forum/threads/annihilation-2026-tournament.432532/

For "Who participated?", distinguish **confirmed finalists**, **played on a named Shotbow video**, and **posted a registration roster**; do not silently promote applicants to actual competitors. For "What place did [clan] finish?", provide **first/second where confirmed**; otherwise say **position not verified** rather than assuming third, quarterfinal, group exit or no-show. If asked for "every clan", explain that the preserved forum excerpts and public recaps are incomplete. Cite relevant **official forum URLs** (or official match videos) instead of asserting access to an unpublished tournament Discord bracket. This history is static as of **22 November 2026**.
