# Shotbow Annihilation dated update notes for Navi

**Reference dates below are the dates shown in the official wiki's release catalog**, which can differ from a forum thread's posting date by a day. Summaries describe what the update introduced _at that time_, not necessarily today's balance. Checked November 2026. Catalog: https://wiki.shotbow.net/Annihilation_Update_Catalog ; news: https://shotbow.net/forum/portal/

## Annihilation updates 2026

**17 October 2026 — Polished Platinum (5.1.0).** A polish and quality-of-life release responding to suggestions and bug reports; notably introduced **three daily and three weekly challenges**, chosen from a pool of 40-plus. The official October 17 forum announcement has more changes; avoid implying this is exclusively a challenge patch. Source: https://shotbow.net/forum/portal/ (17 October 2026 post, “Annihilation Update - Polished Platinum (5.1.0)”).

**27 February 2026 — Annihilation Base 1.21.10 Update (5.0.0).** Shifted Annihilation's server base from Minecraft **1.20.4 to 1.21.10**. Added sword blocking that reduces incoming damage, sped up class switching via the portal, increased render distance and adjusted a boss item. The announcement says the minimum client version became 1.21 and recommends later versions. Source: https://shotbow.net/forum/threads/annihilation-base-1-21-10-update-5-0-0.432382/

## Annihilation updates 2025

**21 December 2025 — Rising Radium (4.2.0).** Restored Annihilation stats leaderboards **in-game**, including monthly leaderboards, alongside boss-system changes, class adjustments, cosmetics and bug fixes. The corresponding forum thread began **20 December**; don't confuse the release-catalog date with the thread date. Sources: https://shotbow.net/forum/threads/annihilation-update-rising-radium-4-2-0.431767/ ; https://shotbow.net/forum/threads/dentrils-new-moon-january-2026.432001/

**21 March 2025 — Chunky Arsenic (4.1.0).** Overhauled clan viewing/joining/management; also updated classes, priority queue, a boss, and game mechanics. These changes are public Annihilation, not FW clan tools. Source: https://shotbow.net/forum/portal/?page=12 (21 March update post and 1 April 2025 Shotbow roundup).

## Annihilation updates 2024

**8 September 2024 — Annihilation Base 1.20.4 Update (4.0.0).** Upgraded from base **1.16 to 1.20.4**, revised multi-attacker PvP damage interactions, revamped stats storage and the /stats GUI, improved class targeting and team doors, and changed in-chat visuals. Old client-version recommendations in this post were superseded by the 2026 base update. Source: https://shotbow.net/forum/threads/annihilation-base-1-20-4-update-4-0-0.426994/

**18 March 2024 — Duel Mode (3.3.0).** Introduced **two-team Red vs Blue** games using compatible duel maps, with automatic activation in lower-population situations; these matches could start with **10 players**. The post described this primarily as a new mode and internal refactor, not a class-balance update. Source: https://shotbow.net/forum/threads/annihilation-update-duel-mode-3-3-0.423826/

## Annihilation updates 2023

**3 October 2023 — Weary Xenon (3.2.0).** Mostly bug fixes: improved map spawn orientation, refactored golden-apple usage and priority-queue logic, improved class ability targeting and made targeted class fixes; restored /logout. The forum post is dated **2 October**. Source: https://shotbow.net/forum/threads/annihilation-update-weary-xenon-3-2-0.422302/

**31 May 2023 — Ambitious Europium (3.1.0).** Class balance and mechanics, notably a **Tank** rework featuring Shield Charge and Devastating Edge, with other improvements and fixes. Source: https://shotbow.net/forum/portal/?page=29 (31 May 2023 Anni Team post).

## Annihilation updates 2022

**25 November 2022 — Annihilation Base 1.16 Update (3.0.0).** A major Minecraft server-base move to **1.16**, laying the groundwork for later features and new blocks; this version was superseded by 1.20.4 in 2024. Source: https://shotbow.net/forum/threads/annihilation-update-base-1-16.418423/

**23 April 2022 — Obvious Osmium (2.16.0).** A named content/balance update featuring **class changes**. Don't infer exact class numbers or mechanics from the title; consult the original changelog for patch-level claims. Catalog: https://wiki.shotbow.net/Annihilation_Update_Catalog

**16 January 2022 — Crispy Cobalt (2.15.0).** Introduced a new game scoreboard, changed **Spider**'s web ability, tightened team priority queue balance, added defense leaderboard reporting and item/enchanted-golden-apple adjustments, and fixed multiple bugs. Some initial reward-withholding changes were explicitly reverted in the thread; do not present the reverted behavior as current. Forum thread began 15 January. Source: https://shotbow.net/forum/threads/annihilation-update-crispy-cobalt-2-15-0.414475/
