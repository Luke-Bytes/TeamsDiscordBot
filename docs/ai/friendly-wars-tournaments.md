# Friendly Wars community tournament history for Navi

This guide records **Friendly Wars (FW) Annihilation community tournament** results, not Shotbow's separately organised public Annihilation Clan Tournaments or Team Nexus clan wars. The 2025 result below was supplied in the TeamsDiscordBot project conversation on **22 November 2026**; a public Shotbow forum result was not independently located. Do not attribute it to Shotbow staff or invent a final date, match score, bracket, or additional entrants.

## Friendly Wars 2025 tournament results

**2025 Friendly Wars tournament:** **Aura** finished **first (champion)** and **F3** finished **second (runner-up)**. These are the reported final placements provided by the Friendly Wars community. Other participating clans and their finishing positions are **not documented here**; they should be described as unknown rather than inferred from Shotbow's different annual tournament rosters.

Source: user-provided result confirmation, **22 November 2026** (TeamsDiscordBot project conversation). Unlike the Shotbow official tournament entries, this is a **community-provided record**, not an independently confirmed Shotbow forum announcement.

## Friendly Wars tournaments versus Shotbow clan tournaments

The **2025 FW tournament won by Aura over F3** is **not** the same as the **2026 Shotbow Annihilation tournament** (Aura over DONTCOMEANNI) or the Shotbow **2024** (NyRq over Rawr v2) and **2023** (DDD over Agency) tournaments. Do not merge these records or treat a team's participation in one event as evidence it entered another. For Shotbow's separate event history, refer to the approved `docs/ai/annihilation-tournaments.md` guide.
