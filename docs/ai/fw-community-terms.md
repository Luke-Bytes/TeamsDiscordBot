# FW community terminology and running jokes for Navi

These definitions come from Friendly Wars community usage, not from a Shotbow staff glossary. Navi can explain the real mechanic and recognise a joke when the question is playful, without turning ordinary gameplay or organiser advice into a roast. Terms can mean something else outside FW.

## Gap drop and leather scouting — FW memes

A **gap drop** or **gap dropping** is when a player dies while carrying a golden apple (gapple) they failed to eat in time. It describes a real missed survival opportunity and is often used to tease someone for wasting a valuable gapple. It does not mean deliberately giving a gapple to another player.

**Leather scouting** means repeatedly charging back in as a fresh-spawn Scout, wearing basic leather armour, often after losing good gear and effectively giving up on gearing again. It can annoy opponents but is usually an ineffective way to kill geared players. The phrase is also used to meme a player who keeps doing it; it is not a separate Annihilation class or an official strategy.

## Deppies and prot12 — FW gearing memes

**Deppies** means boots enchanted with **Depth Strider**. The community jokes about players who obsess over getting deppies even on maps with no water, where that enchantment offers little benefit.

**Prot12**, also written **prot 12**, is shorthand for a full armour set with **Protection III on all four pieces** (3 + 3 + 3 + 3 = 12), typically iron or diamond. It is a desirable benchmark of strong gear, **not** one piece with Protection XII. As a meme, a player might obsess over announcing “I'm ready with prot12!” while the team has no potions or gapples, the Nexus is nearly destroyed, or everyone else needs help. The joke is about selfish over-gearing, not that getting prot12 is bad.

## TP, rift, suc and 6 HP — FW class shorthand

**TP** means the **Transporter** class in FW strategy talk, not a literal teleport command; Transporter creates linked portals for team movement. **Rift** often means the act of using the **Rift Walker** class ability, not a map name or a separate item. **Suc** or **succ** is short for the **Succubus** class or using its finishing ability; in FW callouts, **6 HP** means the target is at six or fewer health points and can be instantly killed by Succubus. Someone saying “he was on 6, I could have succed him” means they missed the chance to finish that target. These are in-game shorthand terms, not Discord commands.

## Organiser, admin and host — community role distinctions

An **organiser** is someone organising a **Friendly Wars** game or event; being an FW organiser **does not make them Shotbow staff**. In FW conversation, an **admin** or **host** generally refers to **official Shotbow staff** supervising or hosting the game/event, rather than an FW organiser. Outside that context, “host” can also mean an ordinary event/game host, so use the surrounding question before assigning someone a staff role. Never infer a real person's verified permissions just from these informal labels.
