# Friendly Wars scenarios - player guide

These are current gameplay instructions, not the original design proposals. The Discord scenario guide uses ScenarioMetadata.ts; keep the descriptions and rules here aligned with it.

## Friendly Wars scenarios - guide, registration and private prompts
Friendly Wars scenarios are optional twists on a normal match: Mystery Dungeon - Personality Test, The People's Captains, Partners in Crime, The Masked Draft, Draft Roulette, Captain's Wildcards and Cursed Classes. Use `/game scenario guide` to browse all seven with a private dropdown, even when there is no announced game. The chosen scenario is explained in the announcement and game feed. Check the current announcement or game feed to find out which scenario you are playing; don't assume one is active. Register normally with `/register`. Watch the game feed and your DMs for any extra instructions. If you closed a prompt, missed a DM or have DMs disabled, press Open My Scenario or run `/game scenario open` in the game server. This opens your own eligible private interaction, not somebody else's. An organiser can resend it to you. Prompts may be edited only while the applicable interaction is available; expired prompts cannot be reopened. The bot attempts reminders for unfinished quizzes, preferences, ballots and class assignments, but DM delivery is not guaranteed. The former developer command named scenario was renamed to debug-scenario; it is unrelated to playing Friendly Wars scenarios.

## Mystery Dungeon - Personality Test
Take a fun personality quiz and see what kind of teammate you are! Your result helps the bot put the teams together. After registering, optionally answer and submit five private multiple-choice questions. Reopening keeps the same questions; you can edit and resubmit before the cutoff, normally 15 minutes before the game. You can keep your personality type private; nobody else gets your individual answers. If you skip or don't finish the quiz, you still play and are included without a classified type. The bot tries to balance team sizes, Elo and personality variety, not put everybody with the same personality together. The quiz is for fun, not a measure of skill or a forced Minecraft class. The organiser confirms the team proposal, then captains are chosen within their teams as usual. Use `/game scenario open` if you missed or closed the quiz.

## The People's Captains
Let the players decide who leads their teams! Everyone who registers can be elected captain. Vote privately for your preferred captain. Every registered player at election opening is a candidate, without volunteering or using captain nomination. Each voter gets three different candidates, never themselves; everyone appears on exactly three ballots. Different voting options are intentional. Pick one name in your DM or use `/game scenario open`. You can change your vote until voting closes; only the latest vote counts. Voting is optional. Normally opens 35 minutes and closes 20 minutes before the game; at least four registered players are needed. The two supported candidates with the most votes win, with ties and colours decided by a fixed draw. At least three votes or 20% of the roster (rounded up), whichever is higher, and two candidates receiving votes are required. Low turnout or roster changes can require organiser review and fallback, not invented winners. The organiser confirms the elected captains, who then draft normally. Individual ballots and votes stay private.

## Partners in Crime
Got a favourite teammate? Pick each other and the bot will try to put you on the same team. After registering, optionally choose one other registered player or No preference privately. Edit using `/game scenario open` until the cutoff, normally 15 minutes before the game. Both players must pick each other to form a duo. Nobody is notified of a one-sided preference. The bot tries to keep mutual pairs together while balancing team sizes and Elo; it's not guaranteed. A successful pairing is publicly named only if both players allow it. If you miss the prompt, have no preference or your choice isn't mutual, you still play. The organiser reviews and confirms teams, then captains are selected within those teams.

## The Masked Draft
Build your team from mystery cards with clues from past matches. Weigh up the evidence before the big reveal! Register normally; only captains make picks. Captains use the team-picking channel to choose anonymous cards in snake draft order. Cards may show shared win-rate bands with today's captains, results or familiarity on particular maps, captain experience, MVP counts or recent form. A card may have two independent clues if enough candidates share that exact combination; others are completely mysterious. Clues use recorded matches from the past year, not self-declared preferences or Elo. Minimum sample sizes are checked internally rather than printed beside win rates. Only already-public captains can be identified in teammate clues. Their current team colour identifies the person, not historical colour performance. Clues are shared across groups of cards and stay fixed during the draft; they do not guarantee future performance. Don't give away identities or try to identify a card from clues. Names are revealed only after the organiser confirms the finished teams. The first pick has two minutes and later picks have one minute each. If the captain runs out of time, the bot chooses a legal card and advances the draft. There is no extra player questionnaire. Captains should use the current draft controls rather than private quiz prompts.

## Draft Roulette
A draft with a little luck thrown in! Captains can only pick from a small selection of players each turn. Register normally; captains pick from a hand of up to three available players in the team-picking channel. Each captain gets one reroll for the entire draft, available only when alternatives exist. A new hand is dealt each turn; otherwise it follows snake draft order. The first pick has two minutes and later picks have one minute each. If you miss your turn, the bot picks from your current hand. The organiser confirms the finished teams. Players don't need to answer anything extra.

## Captain's Wildcards
A normal draft with a twist: each captain gets one special power to shake things up. Register normally. Each captain may use one wildcard during the ordinary draft, or ignore it and pick normally. Veto blocks one undrafted player from the other captain's next pick only; it is offered only when the next pick belongs to the opponent and enough players remain. Switcheroo exchanges one of your own already drafted non-captain teammates for an undrafted player; the released player returns to the pool. Neither power steals an opposing teammate or changes team sizes. Use the private prompt when the bot offers a legal power on your turn, and confirm it to spend your credit. Backing out before confirmation doesn't spend it. The normal pick timer keeps ticking, and unused powers expire; missing the prompt doesn't require a wildcard. No other powers or scenario combinations are available. Keep unconfirmed choices private.

## Cursed Classes
Make life a little harder for the other team by giving three of their players a class they have to stick with! Register normally. Once teams and captains are ready, the bot draws the same three distinct classes for both sides from the organiser's approved eligible pool. Each captain privately assigns each class to a different enemy player, including the enemy captain if desired. Both captains must submit; only then are all six curses revealed. Captains use their private prompt or `/game scenario open` before the cutoff, normally three minutes before the game. Missing assignments or class-access problems require organiser resolution before starting. If cursed, play your assigned class for the whole game, including after death when class selection is available. Other players follow normal class rules. If you don't own or cannot use the assigned class, run `/game scenario report-class` before the game starts. This is an honour rule; the Discord bot cannot enforce or verify class choice in Minecraft. No automatic punishment for suspected violations.
