# Shotbow background for Navi

This is public Shotbow history, not Friendly Wars (FW) match data. Checked against Shotbow's official press kit, wiki, forum, and creator videos in November 2026.

## Shotbow founding and first games

Shotbow dates its founding to **February 2012**. Its official history credits HCGaming LLC owner/operator **Matt Sundberg** and lead developer **Austin Smith** with the early project. **Hardcore Factions (HCFactions)** came first in that team's history; they then developed **MineZ**, a Minecraft zombie-survival game inspired by DayZ. Distinguish “the first game associated with the founders” (Hardcore Factions) from “the breakout early Shotbow game” (MineZ); saying simply “Shotbow's first game was X” hides that distinction. Source: https://shotbow.net/presskit/

MineZ began alpha testing in February 2012, officially released **25 June 2012** according to Shotbow's press kit, and passed **one million unique players by 12 January 2013**. Shotbow grew from MineZ's success into a multi-game network with a shared hub. Other recognisable Shotbow modes have included **Annihilation, SMASH, Ghostcraft and Wasted**. Sources: https://shotbow.net/presskit/sheet.php?p=mineZ and https://wiki.shotbow.net/

**Annihilation opened publicly on 4 July 2013**, according to the Shotbow team's tenth-anniversary post, “Ten Years of Annihilation.” It is a Nexus-defense team PvP game, not a spin-off of the FW Discord bot. Source: https://shotbow.net/forum/threads/ten-years-of-annihilation.421105/

## Fun facts and well-known creators

An early MineZ claim to fame: the Shotbow press kit says its initial zombie AI was coded in **three days** for the February 2012 alpha. In its early days MineZ drew Minecraft YouTube creators; **AntVenom** posted a MineZ video on **22 August 2012**, and **paulsoaresjr** released “Minecraft MineZ - E01 'Kill Paul!'” on **2 July 2012**. These videos substantiate them playing **MineZ**, not specifically Annihilation or today's Shotbow server. Sources: https://shotbow.net/presskit/sheet.php?p=mineZ ; https://youtube.com/watch?v=r4ME1HdzCkE ; https://youtube.com/watch?v=E1XBQGHZhtg

Another community-history detail: Shotbow's official announcement “4 Years Strong” refers to HCFactions as the first game in what would become the network, and describes MineZ as the unexpected breakout. Avoid unsupported famous-player name drops, claims that celebrities played Annihilation, or confusing YouTube coverage with a verified account on today's server. Source: https://shotbow.net/forum/threads/4-years-strong.351031/

## Public Shotbow knowledge versus Friendly Wars

The **public Shotbow Annihilation** wiki describes four-team games, public map rotations, class purchases/unlocks, and periodic game updates. **Friendly Wars** runs community matches with its own registration, team-picking, bans, season stats and organiser commands. Wiki rules, public server commands and class/balance changes must not be presented as guaranteed FW bot rules or live FW state; consult FW guides and read-only tools for that. Shotbow history is static reference material, not evidence of a current FW match. Source: https://wiki.shotbow.net/Annihilation_Game_Summary
