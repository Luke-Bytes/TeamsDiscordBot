# Navi historical tournament matches and streams — catalog and coverage

Reviewed: **22 November 2026**. This document describes the **local, reviewed public evidence archive** consumed by Navi's `search_tournament_matches` tool. It is **not** a complete tournament bracket, unrestricted YouTube search, or a live Friendly Wars database.

## Architecture and editorial safety

- `src/ai/TournamentArchive.json` is the explicitly chosen, repository-local data file bundled statically by TypeScript. Runtime search never selects a path from a user or model, performs network access, downloads footage, or writes records.
- `src/ai/TournamentArchive.ts` defines entities and validates unique IDs, tournament/match/recording relationships, bounded counts, sources restricted to Shotbow forum / YouTube watch URLs, and trusted nonnegative timestamp ranges.
- `src/ai/TournamentMatchSearch.ts` provides local bounded fuzzy ranking. `src/ai/AiTools.ts` exposes a read-only public tool behind **fresh Discord membership verification**; `AiAutoReads` preloads a small result only for plausible archive questions.
- Each tournament has its own organiser, year (or null), registered/seen clans, placements with coverage context, authoritative links and research gaps. Registration is **not** proof of participation.
- Matches separately carry team names, stage, verified map/date/result if available, verification status and claim-specific evidence URLs. **Missing fields are unknown**, never inferred from a later upload date or from final standings.
- Recordings separately carry YouTube IDs, titles, uploader, origin, upload date when identified and a list of match associations. Many-to-many relationships and independently evidenced `startSeconds`/`endSeconds` are supported. When start time is unknown, Navi links the full watch page and explicitly states the segment is not verified.
- Aliases require published evidence and are deliberately conservative. `ANNL` identifies DONTCOMEANNI in the 2026 final, but `F3 Entity` is not silently merged into `F3`; `TP` is not assumed to mean The Family; `TT` is not silently equated with TeamTurkish; historic `rawr` is not automatically `Rawr v2`.

All video/clip references are **external URLs and factual metadata only**; no copyrighted footage or transcripts are redistributed.

### YouTube seed inspection and limitations

- **https://www.youtube.com/@ShotbowOfficial**: public indexed official video results returned titles, upload dates and descriptions for several 2024/2026 games. Direct channel listing pages returned little/no parseable metadata; listings may therefore be incomplete. There may be additional public uploads, replays or unlisted videos.
- **https://www.youtube.com/@lPirlo**: indexed [2023 tournament announcement](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZlVG_sUr4zM), uploaded 1 February 2023. It **does not establish a match recording**. No independently verifiable game/stream association on this channel was identified in accessible search results.
- **https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=icpcNFnAbsU** and **https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4cFKAafjsOA**: direct watch-page inspection returned inaccessible/cache-miss responses and indexed title/metadata searches produced no reliable identification. Their IDs are retained **only as unverified research leads**. Navi must not claim their channels, titles, match opponents, maps, dates or timestamps.
- **Playlists, past livestreams, chapters, match-level timestamp offsets and many brackets**: channel listing and video pages did not reliably expose them through available public indexing. **No timestamped video segment is currently verified.** Do not substitute guessed offsets.

## How to maintain and extend

1. Locate a primary **official match title/description, verified recording segment, or forum bracket/result**. Save exact canonical URL and distinguish the source's claim (teams/stage versus map, score, outcome, date, stream association). For community evidence, record the attribution and keep status partial/uncertain.
2. Add new **tournament** only when its organiser/edition is distinct. Add **match** with a new stable ID and two specifically evidenced sides (never invent a matchup from placements or separate registration posts). Do not make an invented 'final game' from just the champion/runner-up.
3. Add **recording** by YouTube ID. Attach **segments** to match IDs only if the association itself is evidenced. One recording may refer to multiple matches; one match may appear in several recordings. Include `startSeconds` only for a separately validated chapter/timestamp (and optional `endSeconds` greater than start); otherwise omit.
4. Add alias only when the old/new tag equivalence is demonstrated. Similar strings may be included as **lower-confidence search suggestions** but are not a verified identity merge.
5. Run `npm run typecheck`, `npm run tests`, `npx prettier --check src/ai/Tournament*.ts src/ai/TournamentArchive.json tests/cases/aiTournamentArchive.test.ts`; review diff and ensure a question about **current FW gameplay** is not automatically prefetched with this archive.
6. Update this coverage table, source log, review date, known inaccessible links and explicit gaps after research. Updating the catalog is a **separate editorial/repository action**, never a capability given to Navi or a user-facing live query.

### Response expectations

When two named clans are not found together, reply **“I don't have an indexed match proving that pairing,”** not “they never played.” If the user recalls a map unsupported by sources, show the best **team-based** alternatives and state that the map is unknown or contradicts the memory. For a broad year/stage search, say **“these are the identified games, not the complete schedule.”** Always give the most useful video watch link and only attach a `&t=` timestamp when it is verified. For 2025 FW, first and second place are community-provided information, not an independently documented final game.
