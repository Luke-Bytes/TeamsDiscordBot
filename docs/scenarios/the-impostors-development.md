## 1. Decisions and review findings

### Confirmed by the user

- Shotbow has given permission for these activities during the custom game. Do not treat ordinary public-match restrictions as a blocker for that authorised environment. Do not extend the exception to public Annihilation or unrelated grief.

- Preserve the eleven physical mission descriptions exactly. Do not add new missions, reinterpret them, introduce additional requirements or impose the earlier review's proposed physical caps/limits/proof criteria. Opportunity differs by role/map, but this is acceptable; offer private rerolls rather than over-engineering role-based allocation.
- Teams have at least eight players each, usually ten to twelve each, sometimes more. No arbitrary maximum of twenty.
- All six proposed voice sabotage types are in scope, including server mute/deafen, Communications Split, Solitary Confinement and Leaderless. Random groups are the preference where applicable; the single-player/captain effects are explicit exceptions. Effects last 1-3 minutes. Optional delayed activation and automatic scheduling should require little/no midgame Discord interaction.
- Use the scheduled announcement time as the practical timing reference: game probably starts about three minutes after announced start (e.g. 13:00 -> approximately 13:03), usually within five minutes. No additional organiser start/phase confirmation needed. This is an estimate, not observed phase telemetry.
- A simple in-memory record of who the bot muted/deafened is appropriate. Moderator overlap is unlikely in this custom game; spectators require only a simple eligibility check. Do not defer the effects or create extensive Impostors-specific crash recovery. Still clean up normally and account for existing moderation state.
- Extend the scenario architecture to support live and post-game activity; make voice sabotage and voting reusable in future scenarios. Preserve existing seven scenarios and normal game workflow.
- No screensharing in team voice chat, preferably by channel permissions. Final team discussion 3-4 minutes.
- Physical mission completion is self-reported by impostors. Community challenges/evidence are informal and voluntary; no bot-managed evidence intake, independent witnesses, screenshot requirements, verification thresholds, review queues or mandatory organiser adjudication.
- Reveal the claimed completed missions after voting closes, then reveal the impostors after a short opportunity for speculation. No proof or roles before voting locks.

## 2. Player experience

1. Organiser announces a custom Friendly War with The Impostors, with the twist, voice disruptions, no screen sharing, permitted physical missions and rating disclosed before registration. Normal team selection, captains and plans continue. One scenario per match initially.
2. Once RED/BLUE rosters are final, randomly assign impostors independently on each team. Everyone receives a comparable private role card; innocent card just says play normally, spot suspicious behaviour and predicts/votes if desired. Impostor card includes five missions, optional reroll and their voice assignment/window. No public role, per-player mission status or role-specific DM failure notices.
3. All players may make a private First Impressions prediction before play. It is optional, does not delay start, does not affect final vote or normal result. No public early results.
4. Game proceeds normally. Impostors try to finish any four of their five missions without giving themselves away, and may activate their assigned voice sabotage with a quick private control. Bot auto-activates an unspent compulsory slot. No innocent objectives, compulsory live reading/typing, video recording or formal midgame meetings.
5. On actual game end, stop pending sabotages, restore active ones, keep team VCs separate for four minutes of discussion, then open private final ballots. Normal MVP/winner workflow remains compatible. No mission disclosure before both ballots lock.
6. Each impostor privately ticks the physical missions they say they completed. No evidence upload or adjudication queue. At deadline lock the claims, then show each team's anonymous completed-mission list (reported, not independently verified). Let teammates speculate briefly, then reveal identities and results. Voting cannot change after this reveal. Community can challenge claims and ask for evidence informally; bot does not referee. No new mandatory task for innocents or organisers.
7. Final reveal: normal winner, impostors on both sides, their claimed missions/voice assignment result and deduction, partial victories, optional First Impressions comparison and fun awards. Ordinary shutdown/persistence resumes once scenario scoring is complete or explicitly skipped and effects cleaned.

## 5. Reusable voice sabotage

Implement all six effect types using a shared scenario voice effect service with generic inputs (match token, effect ID, team/channel, eligible participants, duration, selection policy, optional captain ID) and results (targets, started/ended, applied/failed/void, reason). No Impostors role semantics in reusable adapter. Keep scheduling, role/slot entitlement and scoring inside Impostors or shared runtime helper, not Discord effect code.

| Effect | Target selection | Duration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Radio Silence | Random ~30% of eligible connected team members, server mute | 3 min |
| Blackout | Random ~20% of eligible connected team members, server deafen | 1 min |
| Quarantine | 2-3 random eligible team members moved to a temporary private same-team VC | 2 min |
| Communications Split | Randomly partition eligible team members into two same-team groups; one group remains, the other moves to a temporary same-team VC | 2 min |
| Solitary Confinement | One random eligible team member moved to a temporary same-team VC | 3 min |
| Leaderless | Current frozen team's captain, server mute | 2 min |

All six available for random assignment. No deferment or replacement effects. Leaderless deliberately targets the captain and Solitary Confinement is single-player; other targets chosen randomly without names typed. The effect does not reveal its impostor actor. For percentages use rounded count with min 1 and sensible cap preserving some unaffected members; e.g. 10 connected eligible -> 3 mute, 2 deafen. Quarantine -> 2 for smaller connected groups, 3 for ten or more (proposed); leave others able to communicate. Split groups as evenly as possible; random which stays. Never mix enemy teams. If insufficient eligible targets, missing captain or inaccessible resource, mark slot void rather than silently select an unrelated person. All effects must work in offline tests; actual Discord behaviour subject to normal permissions and separately authorised staging validation.

## 6. Reusable voting and deduction

Make a generic private ballot/tally module usable by future scenarios (not tied to impostor roles). Configurable eligible voter/candidate IDs, grouping/team, max selections, self-vote policy, edit/deadline, quorum and tie policy; stable owner/match/action binding. Pure tally functions return counts, definite selected candidates and unresolved seats; callers map to role-aware outcomes. Don't replace People's Captains or other current voting implementations in this task; reuse suitable helpers, retain their semantics.

First Impressions: optional private prediction after role assignment, before bot start/estimated play (lock when ordinary game start completes so no live input). Same selection capacity as final, no self/other-team, no live results. Does not affect actual match, final vote or scoring, only fun comparisons. No required reminder.

Final discussion four minutes in separate team VCs; allow team text participation, no demands for role proof or mandatory speech. Then 90-second final ballot (proposed; originally one minute). Each frozen eligible participant including impostors can choose up to N distinct teammates (N = assigned impostor count on their own team). One vote per selected suspect, no self, duplicates, enemy, nonparticipant, stale ID. Explicit submit/abstain, edit until deadline, private responses. Same role-independent UI and error messages for everyone. More than 25 candidates use pagination/multi-page selection and review/submit atomically, not reject larger teams. No public live totals or individual votes, no public turnout by role. Generic deadline and reminders. Never reveal missions or evidence before both team ballots are locked.

Recommended tally:
1. Frozen denominator R = original team's eligible roster. Majority positive-ballot quorum floor(R/2)+1 (one valid nonempty ballot per eligible voter). Explicit abstain distinct from absent; neither establishes positive quorum. No reduction for disconnect/strategic unregister. If no quorum, team deduction inconclusive; no hidden/exposed credit from invalid tally.
2. Count each selected suspect once per ballot. Rank positive counts; fill N seats from highest down. If tied group fits remaining seats, all are definite accused; if tied group straddles cutoff, leave those seats unresolved (no random ID tiebreak). No runoff recommended for v1; can reuse configurable policy later. Fewer positive suspects than N => incomplete.
3. Valid quorum + N definite accused = complete. Definite accused impostor exposed; with incomplete tally, unaccused impostor is inconclusive, not confirmed hidden. In complete tally, unaccused impostor stayed hidden. Innocents' deduction result is how many true impostors exposed; full/partial/none on valid complete tally, unresolved otherwise. Team deduction independent of match result.
4. Examples: 10 players, N=2, six valid positive ballots, scores A=5 B=4 C=4 -> A definitely accused, one seat unresolved; 5 positive plus 2 abstains -> no quorum; six positive, A=5 B=4 C=1 -> A/B definite. For a team of eight, N=1, five positive ballots but top tied -> unresolved.
5. Fun awards optional: Psychic (all early guesses correct), Sixth Sense (at least one early correct), Master Detective (all final correct), Easily Fooled (early correct but missed final, private or consented), Master of Deception (all missions complete and zero final votes in valid complete deduction), Red Herring (most suspected innocent, optional and consented). No public shame labels, raw ballots or secret status before lock. Awards don't affect normal game or scenario scoring. Public high-level comparison/consented highlights only.

## 7. Postgame mission claims and staged reveal

No formal community verification. No evidence intake, witness nomination, independent reviewer selection, screenshot requirement, attachment handling, moderation scoring service, automated proof or bot-enforced dispute deadline. Players may save screenshots or offer testimony voluntarily if challenged; existing community moderation handles serious misconduct. A claim is a self-reported honour result, not verified. This deliberately accepts the possibility of exaggeration or collusion in a casual custom game; don't introduce verification friction to prevent it.

Recommended UI: at final ballot lock, each impostor privately sees their five mission labels with multi-select/tick controls and Submit. Empty submission allowed; updates until 90s deadline. Owner/token/stage validation; only assigned IDs; no duplicated/repeated credit, no role leak in public notices. Missing submission = no claimed missions, not proof of misconduct. Bot reads voice results from service, not claimant. Do not require innocent player responses to finish.

### Staged reveal

1. Both final ballots closed; lock claims at deadline (may finish earlier only if no timing/role-count leak; fixed deadline preferred).
2. Publish separate anonymous team lists of claimed completed missions, e.g. "Red team - missions reported complete: Confession, Potion Waster, Fake Gapple, Hoe Donation." If duplicate mission claims, list count (e.g. x2); don't group by actor or expose timing, names, raw claim form or vote counts. Only show teams their own list until full reveal if helpful; shared reveal can show both team lists if players already moved to shared VC. No official accusations/roles yet. Clearly say these are reported claims, not bot-verified.
3. Short speculation period (~45s proposed) for fun. Ballots are permanently locked; no late changes to deduction after seeing missions. No formal evidence action, no mandatory challenge/response. Players can speculate or offer voluntary evidence in ordinary voice/chat.
4. Final reveal identities, which missions each claims, sabotage assignment result, definite accusations, deduction and partial victory; early predictions fun summary. Players may challenge claims informally and ask impostors to show evidence, even after reveal. Do not expose private evidence or individual ballots through bot. The bot does not judge a challenge, halt shutdown, or revise score automatically. If exceptional misconduct requires correction, normal authorised organiser/community handling outside this lightweight scoring process; don't add formal dispute UI. If user wants post-reveal correction capability later, specify it separately.

Nominal total after /game end = 4 min discussion + 90s vote + 90s claims + 45s speculation = 7m45s, plus small setup. Bound each phase and provide safe organiser skip. No requirement that all players stay until end; timeouts close automatically. Ordinary MVP prompts may run during later phases after vote lock; maintain team separation until ballots locked. Do not start shared VC movement before then.

## 8. Scoring

Keep normal Annihilation result and scenario result independent. Winning own team remains core; no Elo bonuses, persistent leaderboards or reward for losing. Normal winner authoritative; no need to sacrifice defence or extend game for a mission. Four of five distinct self-reported physical missions satisfies physical assignment. Five gives no extra scoring reward. Compulsory voice slot successful application is separate. Recommended combined assignment:
- PASS when 4+ physical claims and voice slot fulfilled (manual or fallback).
- FAIL when fewer than four claimed or deliberate noncompletion established by normal rule enforcement; no automated misconduct inference from nonresponse.
- INCONCLUSIVE when physical quota met but slot never safely reached/applied due to short game/technical problem. Do not penalise normal result or manufacture perfect victory. Report actual claimed count and voice status regardless.
- Scenario void on aborted/invalid match, role-integrity failure or unresolved critical technical failure. Unknown winner distinct from loss; no perfect label until winner confirmed. No automatic quota reduction for impossible card; rerolls pregame and spare card mitigate.

| yes | yes | yes | Perfect Impostor Victory |

| yes | no | yes | Won the War, Assignment Incomplete |
| yes | no | no | Won the War, Exposed |

If any axis unknown/inconclusive, show known achievements and neutral outcome, not binary fabricated label. All innocent players have normal match result plus team deduction/optional detective achievements, no assignment. Full perfect victory never granted with failed/inconclusive mission or vote. Losing impostor partial result is descriptive, not competitive bonus.

Winner may be reported before/after end; preserve scoring axes at lock, compute combined label when normal winner known (if not yet known, show pending). Normal winner correction updates only combined result before save. No replay votes/claims. Public metadata contains approved summary (role reveal, reported mission count, effect status, deduction, outcomes), no raw private ballots/evidence or secret assignments before reveal. No Elo/gameType changes beyond explicitly approved rating policy. Recommend unrated initially because disruptions affect competitive play, but do not enforce new policy without confirmation.

## 9. Lifecycle and reusable architecture

Extend ScenarioTypes and ScenarioCoordinator with optional runtime/postgame capability and explicit action/stage policy. Current preparation phase is still completed to pass normal game start; separate runtime state or stage type for live/postgame rather than weakening isLive() globally. | Runtime stage | Entry / responsibility |
| --- | --- |

| result-ready | Reveal roles/results; safe shutdown/persistence. |

Use token/revision/stage/deadline checks, fixed server deadlines, idempotent transitions, shared roster lock for roster/start/end/shutdown decisions and effect reservation before async REST. Don't wait under lock for callback reacquiring it. End invalidates activation before awaiting cleanup. Stale interaction cannot affect next game. Ordinary game flow not held indefinitely. No separate game manager.
